What this is. A digital twin is a live software model of a physical process, driven by sensor data. This proof-of-concept simulates a four-station line and mirrors each station's state, load, temperature, vibration and waiting time in real time. On top of the twin sits the idea proposed in the SHAPE-5 research: instead of a fixed alarm, the system reads each station's context and chooses a proportionate level of support — from quietly monitoring, through informing and warning, up to recommending an action or escalating — and records why each change was made. Use Inject fault to see support escalate as conditions worsen. This is an illustrative simulation, not a validated system.
